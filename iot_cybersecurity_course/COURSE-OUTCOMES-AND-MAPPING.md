# Outcomes, Observable Evidence, Quality & Gap Map

## Overall outcomes (proposed)
1. Explain IoT device/gateway/cloud architecture and identify its safety/privacy-sensitive attack surface — M01–M03.
2. Operate an isolated IoT assessment lab and maintain evidence provenance — M04–M06.
3. Analyze IoT traffic, MQTT and wireless security controls — M07–M12.
4. Assess hardware/firmware exposures without unsafe interaction — M13–M20.
5. Evaluate secure boot, OTA, identity, cloud and lifecycle controls — M21–M23 and M29.
6. Investigate botnets, preserve evidence, build timelines and detections — M24–M27.
7. Explain IIoT/OT safety and risk reduction — M28.
8. Produce an ethical, traceable portfolio capstone and SOC handoff — M30.

## Evidence-oriented learning assessment (no quizzes)
- Basics: architecture diagram, asset map, threat register.
- Traffic/protocol: PCAP annotations, MQTT controls and secure protocol comparison.
- Firmware: hashes, extraction inventory, redacted security finding, reverse-engineering notes.
- Defense: device identity, OTA pipeline, lifecycle profile and SIEM detection rules.
- Investigation: chain of custody, forensic timeline, incident triage and handoff.
- Capstone: scope, three evidence-backed findings, controls, retest and report.

## Current content gaps and honest limitations
- Full 120 distinct lesson-specific YouTube videos were **not** found or individually audited. Some modules share a cross-cutting tutorial; each module nevertheless has one or more actual identified YouTube links.
- Deep BLE, Zigbee, CoAP, cloud-IoT and OTA topics need instructor-authored examples and preferably additional verified topical video sources before production launch.
- Lab scripts, screenshots, sample PCAPs and test firmware files are not bundled, to avoid redistributing third-party assets or risk-bearing binaries.
- The YouTube playlist from the professor is publisher-linked but individual playlist entries were not checked.
- No written pre-existing institutional course files were supplied, so this is a new **original draft** rather than a licensed redesign of third-party course materials.
