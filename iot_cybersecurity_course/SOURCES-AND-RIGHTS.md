## Link and media provenance

- Video page titles/metadata were obtained from public search listings; selected source examples include Engineering Funda, LufSec, Atdhe Buja, Wild West Hackin Fest, Suit Up and Hack, and USENIX.
- The author site https://www.atdheb.com/video-iot-forensics-series-a-complete-university-level-course/ documents a 17-part IoT forensics collection and an Industrial IoT YouTube playlist.
- The 2026 revision of NIST 8259 is at https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8259/r1/final. Baseline NIST IR 8259A remains separately linked.
- The OWASP IoT Security Testing Guide https://owasp.org/owasp-istg/01_introduction/index.html and IoTGoat https://github.com/OWASP/IoTGoat inform safe practice structure.
- The IoT-OPEN materials at https://iot-open.eu/mooc-materials-on-iot-for-tutors/ state **CC BY-NC** licensing. This curriculum does not copy those materials; commercial reuse requires reviewing their terms.

## Publication policy

1. Link to YouTube watch pages or embeds as permitted by YouTube and rights holders; do not redistribute YouTube files, thumbnails or transcripts without permission.
2. Attribute the creator and preserve the original canonical URL.
3. Confirm any external lab image license, reuse restrictions and whether an asset is intended for deliberate exploitation.
4. Keep a video audit log: module, URL, publisher, check date, topical fit, embed test, captions, replacement reason.
5. Treat externally hosted resources as changeable: check links on release and periodically afterward.
