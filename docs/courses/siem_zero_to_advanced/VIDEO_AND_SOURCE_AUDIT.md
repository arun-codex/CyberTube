## Quality controls and limitations

- All 30 module files include 4 lesson objectives/activities, at least one explicit external YouTube URL, a guided lab, evidence requirement, and knowledge questions.
- Long videos are reused across modules at chapter-level timestamps; this makes the course watchable without inventing videos for highly specific topics.
- The YouTube **playlist itself is curated as Markdown links**, not a YouTube-created playlist. No channels or videos are represented as produced by this author.
- A YouTube video may omit the exact full exercise; the companion module provides independent original lab instructions and vendor docs.
- Some Microsoft Community videos are older than the latest Sentinel portal experience; cross-check current docs and 2026 SC-200 episodes.
- No live Azure/Splunk deployment has been done or validated as part of authoring.
- External media rights have **not** been cleared for downloading, mirroring or rehosting. Use external links until permissions are obtained.
- Under the separate gated Agentic Course Redesign workflow, no source eligibility, Gate 0 approvals, formal redesign production or final acceptance have been recorded; this pack is a standalone independently authored draft, not an accepted release through that protected workflow.
