# YouTube Video Library — SIEM (curated external videos)

**Read this first:** Videos are hosted by third parties; the package links to them, does not own, re-upload or redistribute them. Each module includes direct YouTube videos, sometimes reusing relevant chapters of longer tutorials. The course sequence itself is a custom curated learning path, **not a YouTube playlist uploaded under this title**. Video links were drawn from YouTube search listings or links on the official Splunk/Microsoft training pages; availability can change.
