## Core citations and interpretation
- NIST SP 800-61 Rev.3 is the primary **current** incident-response publication (final April 2025).
- CISA federal IR playbooks provide operational examples and were originally written against the older NIST lifecycle; use in context.
- MITRE ATT&CK is a behavior taxonomy, not proof that an observed event is malicious.
- Threat-intel enrichment results are time-dependent and can produce false positives.
- Some sample malware traffic PCAPs can contain embedded exploit content; use isolated training systems only.
