# Suggested 16-Week Study Plan

Estimated independent learner workload: ~8–10 hours per week. Some advanced labs may require additional time. Weekly routine: ~3 h video and note-taking, ~3 h guided labs, ~1–2 h report, ~1–2 h revision/quiz.

## Weekly template
- Monday: Watch primary videos and note unfamiliar terms.
- Tuesday: Complete first two module lessons.
- Wednesday: Complete remaining lesson prompts.
- Thursday–Friday: Work the simulated lab and collect evidence.
- Saturday: Write an analyst report, complete review questions and correct errors.
- Sunday: Revisit mistakes, review terminology and simulate a shift handoff.

## Milestones
- After Week 3: Explain SOC IR lifecycle and find authentication evidence.
- After Week 6: Produce an escalation-ready alert triage ticket.
- After Week 10: Reconstruct cross-source timeline and evidence ledger.
- After Week 14: Explain containment, recovery and cloud IR decisions.
- After Week 16: Deliver a documented end-to-end fictional incident case.
