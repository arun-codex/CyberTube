# Cybersecurity Incident Response — Zero to Advanced (YouTube-First)

> **Defensive/authorized use only.** Work exclusively on owned or explicitly authorized systems, synthetic logs, and freely distributable training images. Do not execute live malware, run destructive actions, or upload sensitive enterprise evidence to public services. Security controls, response approvals and legal notification duties are organization/jurisdiction specific.

> **Current framework:** NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 (final April 3, 2025) aligns IR with NIST CSF 2.0. Historical PICERL / four-phase terminology in older videos is taught for mapping only; Rev 2 is superseded.

**Audience:** Complete beginners to aspiring SOC L1/L2 and DFIR responders. **Course format:** 30 modules, 150 guided lesson topics, 30 hands-on assignments, one capstone. **Study estimate:** 12–16 weeks at roughly 8–10 hours per week (estimate, not video runtime).


**Cost model:** YouTube lessons and the included original Markdown materials are free to access. External learning platforms may restrict selected labs to paid tiers and cloud environments may incur charges; offline synthetic-data alternatives are included.


## Start here

1. Read `LAB_SETUP_AND_SAFETY.md` and create an isolated analysis VM (optional for paper/data labs).
2. Open `COURSE_INDEX.md` and take modules in order.
3. For **each** module: watch direct YouTube videos, read free resource links, complete the five lesson prompts, complete the practical lab and save the evidence artifact.
4. Use the reusable Markdown templates in `templates/`.
5. Run the capstone scenario and grade your work using `ASSESSMENT_AND_RUBRIC.md`.
6. Save a sanitized portfolio of your own reports; never upload private employer logs.

## What you will know at completion

- Validate, prioritize, investigate, document and escalate SOC alerts.
- Correlate Windows/Linux/network/cloud telemetry and preserve evidence.
- Recommend approval-aware containment, eradication and recovery.
- Explain limitations, open questions, response decisions and business impact.
- Produce credible incident reports and operational handoffs.

## Important notes about YouTube

Every module has **two direct YouTube watch links**. Some resources intentionally recur because one longer workshop supports different modules; watch the relevant chapter and focus on the module-specific assignment. A third topical YouTube search link helps find more recent videos but is **not presented as a verified video**. Videos are linked rather than downloaded or copied. Creator availability/titles may change.
