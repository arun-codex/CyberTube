# STUDY_SCHEDULE — 24 weeks, Linux-only

**Recommended rhythm:** Monday–Saturday: 25–35 min assigned video, 55–65 min hands-on terminal work, 15 min notes, 10 min retrieval quiz. Sunday: rest or review. Approximately 9–12 hours/week.

## Daily routine
1. **Preview:** Read objectives and write down 3 questions.
2. **Watch:** Play the direct YouTube video, pause every 3–8 minutes, reproduce the commands.
3. **Practice:** Complete the module’s lab without copying the solution verbatim.
4. **Document:** Record exact commands, output, failures and corrected commands.
5. **Test:** Answer 3 knowledge questions without opening the video.

## Milestones
- **End of Week 5:** Navigate Linux shell, edit files and analyze text.
- **End of Week 10:** Administer users, permissions, packages, processes and systemd.
- **End of Week 14:** Manage storage safely, perform restores and schedule jobs.
- **End of Week 17:** Diagnose Linux networking, SSH and firewall access.
- **End of Week 20:** Write robust Bash scripts and investigate Linux logs.
- **End of Week 24:** Deliver a documented Linux server capstone.

## Suggested scoring
- Videos and notes: 10%
- Lab correctness and demonstrated commands: 50%
- Quiz and explanations: 20%
- Challenge task: 20%
- Target: >=80%, with 100% completion of safety steps in storage/SSH/firewall labs.

## Retention method
Use a spaced review on Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 and Day 21 after a module. Type five remembered commands from scratch; do not just reread notes.
