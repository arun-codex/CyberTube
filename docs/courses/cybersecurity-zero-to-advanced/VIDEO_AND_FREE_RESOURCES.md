## Video-link honesty convention
- **Direct video/playlist**: known specific YouTube resource linked and identified by title.
- **YouTube topic discovery**: a search-results link for the *specific module*. It is not a verified playlist or endorsement of the results.
- The Professor Messer playlist is a recurring common backbone and **does not independently teach every advanced specialty**. Pair it with the indicated specialist video/search, official reading and hands-on exercises.
- Preview each video and verify it still exists before publishing the curriculum inside a website or LMS.
