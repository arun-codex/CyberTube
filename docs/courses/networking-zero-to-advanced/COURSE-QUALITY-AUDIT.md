## Quality Scoring Methodology
Every lesson video was audited against 6 strict dimensions:
1. **Topic Alignment (40 pts)**: Direct coverage of the lesson's target protocol or security topic.
2. **Learning Objective Coverage (25 pts)**: Teaches core concepts, packet structures, and observables.
3. **Technical Accuracy (15 pts)**: Correct protocol standards (RFC/IEEE), byte offsets, and behaviors.
4. **Educational Quality (10 pts)**: Clear visual diagrams, clear voice audio, and structured progression.
5. **Difficulty Alignment (5 pts)**: Matches the learner's skill progression across the 30-module journey.
6. **Practical Usefulness (5 pts)**: Real-world packet analysis, Wireshark filters, or SOC observables.
