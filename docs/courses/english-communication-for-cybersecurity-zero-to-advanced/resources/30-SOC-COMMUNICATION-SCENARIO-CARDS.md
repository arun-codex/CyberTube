# 30 Cybersecurity English Role-Play Cards

> All cases are **invented** and intended only for language practice. Hosts and IPs are examples. Use no live organisation or person data.

## Card 01: Audio clarity
**Scenario:** A teammate mishears “firewall” as “file wall”.
**Learner instruction:** Read security terms at a natural pace, then break them into syllables if misunderstood.
**Listener prompt 1:** “What do you know for sure?”
**Listener prompt 2:** “What do you still need to confirm?”
**Success check:** Exact word and a confirmed repeat.
**Follow-up:** Rephrase the answer for a nontechnical person in no more than 35 words.
