## Important distinctions

- English education resources teach language; NIST and MITRE provide technical vocabulary and context only.
- BBC Learning English, Oxford Online English and English with Lucy have free public-facing YouTube lessons. Paid courses, tutoring and ads are not required to use the links listed here.
- Source and video title discovery was via public search on **2026-11-21**; links might later change. Embedded playback, transcripts, geographic availability, access and redistribution rights are **not independently certified**.
- Learner artifacts are *original simulated communications*, not summaries of copyrighted video scripts. Do not copy transcripts into course files.
- The NIST incident response reference used is **SP 800-61 Revision 3**, final 2025-04-03; review current local policy when teaching a specific workplace.
- Avoid invented CVEs, threat actor attribution, IP ownership, victim personal information and internal incident details.

## Source-review protocol before production

1. Open the direct YouTube watch URL and confirm actual playback, channel, title and English communication topic.
2. Confirm that relevant portions support the module objective and captions are accurate or accessible alternatives exist.
3. Check dates, video availability and playlist membership; mark any blocked link “REPLACE”, not “VERIFIED”.
4. Record creator, link, check date, licensing/embedding status (separately), and the reviewer’s initials.
5. Reassess general-English-to-SOC instructional transfer through a learner pilot and a technical reviewer.
