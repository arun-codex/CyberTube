## Pedagogical fit and limits

- Primary video links were individually surfaced by YouTube-indexed search results with named creators. Search results can verify discoverability/title, not actual playback or licensing for rehosting.
- Playlist identities were cross-checked through indexed playlist listings, with selected playlist URLs opened at YouTube. Individual episode ordering and caption availability may change.
- A **small number of primary videos repeat** in related modules for purposeful application and progressive practice. There are 30 modules, not 30 unique videos. Some module topics (e.g. incident escalation) are specialised applications of a general business-English video.
- No videos claim to be produced for this course and no paid/language course certificates are advertised.
- Review every module’s specific video-to-goal fit before publication, and replace weak matches with more precise teaching videos following the same source-check protocol.
- Recommended viewing protocol: watch free videos on YouTube and use captions where provided; do not upload YouTube audio/video to Content Studio.
