# Syllabus and complete lesson map

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course learners should be able to:
1. Speak intelligible basic workplace English and accurately pronounce technical identifiers.
2. Listen to cybersecurity conversations and confirm unknown details without guessing.
3. Write coherent factual SOC tickets, emails, handovers and risk summaries.
4. Deliver briefings to technical and nontechnical audiences with measured certainty.
5. Demonstrate communication skills during simulated SOC incidents and interviews.

## Workload and pedagogy

Each lesson is planned for 40 minutes of contact or guided self-study, plus approximately 10–20 minutes of review. A learner may need significantly longer at lower English levels. 120 lessons × 40 minutes = 80 guided hours, before independent work; offer alternative pacing.

Avoid mandatory quizzes or gated unlocks. Support multimodal accessible practice, subtitles and independent formative feedback.
