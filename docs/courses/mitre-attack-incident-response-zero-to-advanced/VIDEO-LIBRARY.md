## Editorial checks before publishing

- Open **every** URL and confirm target, creator, playback and no region/age/fee gate; record date and replacement.
- Evaluate every chapter/objective against video **transcripts or actual viewing**. Broad playlists need specific required episode selection after review.
- Old ATT&CK vocabulary must be updated using v20.1; especially Stealth and Defense Impairment from 2026.
- The videos are third-party works. Link or use permissible embeds; do not download and republish.
- No single verified playlist spans all 30 incident-response modules. This is a **curated curriculum assembled from several official series**, plus explanatory practice links.
