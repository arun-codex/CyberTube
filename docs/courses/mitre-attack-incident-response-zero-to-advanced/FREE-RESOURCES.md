## Safe practice defaults

1. Prefer synthetic case records generated by the instructor for basic exercises.
2. Use public security datasets only under their own licences and after verifying they contain no secrets.
3. For malware-related lessons, limit beginners to non-executable log and metadata analysis.
4. Simulate irreversible response actions in written exercises.
5. Keep the evidence ledger separate from the analyst interpretation.
